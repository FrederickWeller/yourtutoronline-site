Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026. This is a general, good-faith privacy policy and isn't a substitute for legal advice.

Information We Collect

We collect information only when it's necessary to provide our tutoring services.

Contact information: your name and email address, when you inquire about tutoring or book a free intro session.

Enrollment data: when you enroll in tutoring, your name, email, billing address, and (if provided) phone number, used for scheduling and communication.

Payment data: all payments are processed directly by Stripe; we don't see or store your full card number.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide tutoring support and materials via TutorBird and Google Workspace, send class reminders and schedule updates by email, and process payments and receipts. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to anyone.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and may collect information such as your IP address and browser type. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings. See Google's Privacy Policy for more.

Third-Party Services

We work with a small number of service providers to run this business, including TutorBird (scheduling and client management), Stripe (payment processing), Google Workspace (email and document collaboration), and Google Analytics (site analytics). Each has its own privacy practices governing the information they process on our behalf. We will never sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties.

Children's Privacy

This website and our tutoring services are intended for community college students and above, and working professionals — not children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Security and International Operations

As a U.S.-based business operated from Colombia, your information is processed according to U.S. data protection standards, using secure, encrypted connections (SSL/TLS).

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide tutoring services, maintain business records, and meet legal or tax obligations.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or unsubscribe from non-essential emails, at any time by emailing frederick@yourtutoronline.com.

Changes to This Policy
